I'm a physicist working on **statistical physics, network science and synchronization**.
I'm a PhD student at the [Doctoral School of Physical Sciences, BME](https://phd.physics.bme.hu/)
and a researcher in the Complex Systems Laboratory of the HUN-REN Centre for Energy Research,
supervised by Dr. Géza Ódor.

My main topic is how **heterogeneity** (in network structure and in parameters) shapes collective
dynamics. In practice, that mostly means modelling high-voltage power grids with the
second-order Kuramoto model: synchronization, cascade failures, grid reinforcement, HVDC links,
and lately linear stability and fixed-point analysis of the continental European grid together
with Pere Colet and Damià Gomila at [IFISC](https://ifisc.uib-csic.es/).
I write a lot of simulation code for CPUs and GPUs, and I also work with machine-learning models
for image recognition and monitoring.
